# Quick card handoffs

Brian requested a quick card action for giving or trading cards to a named person outside MTG Archives, with permanent history, and for giving cards to an existing user. He explicitly chose immediate delivery into the recipient's Unassigned location on the sender's confirmation. Ordinary negotiated trades retain their existing acceptance and two-person confirmation flow.

In Inventory, use **Give / trade away** from a card's actions menu or details. Copies default to one. Choose an Archive user or enter an outside person's name; recent outside names are suggested. A source selector appears when the card represents multiple physical lots, so the location, section, finish, condition and language remain clear. The final button is the single confirmation. Outside handoffs remove only the specified copies; Archive handoffs transfer them immediately into Unassigned as a separate provenance lot.

**Gifts and traded-away history** is available from Inventory, the success message and negotiated Trade history. Records include printing, copies, attributes, recipient, date and gift/trade designation. Received cards have their own history view. Receipts survive later inventory deletion and catalog changes. Names of outside people, source notes and private storage are not exposed through another user's received-history view. Other owners require explicit Admin Mode.

Live permission, source revision and unreserved quantity checks run inside the stock transaction. Committed cards, including League deck commitments, must first use the deck return workflow. Loose cards retain ordinary trade eligibility. Actual opener, notes, condition, language, finish, pull and round provenance are retained. Ordinary trade reservations remain valid. Request identity and immutable receipts prevent duplicate delivery, including concurrent clicks and a lost response. An unresolved confirmation remains available for a safe same-request retry after closing/reloading.
